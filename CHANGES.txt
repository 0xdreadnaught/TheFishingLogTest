The Fishing Log (Test) - build 2

Record sync fixes:
- Whisper follow-ups return to the peer, including when guild/group routes
  are absent. The existing record tally also travels during whisper swaps.
- Routine books relay a rotating slice of all known species records, including
  records held by offline anglers. Own records and spot Kings retain priority.
  Books remain capped at 80 entries; send rate and peer-discovery limits stay
  the same. Rotation is per peer and bounded to 64 remembered cursors.
- Large multipart transfers expire after two minutes without new parts rather
  than two minutes from their start. Duplicate parts do not extend that timer;
  a 30-minute absolute lifetime and existing size/count caps remain in place.
- /tfl diag includes queued packets and completed/expired transfer counts.

Existing RBOOK/RBOOK2/RTALLY formats are preserved. Regression checks verify
legacy receivers, offline-holder relay, independent peer progress, cooldowns,
packet order/duplicates, and transfer timeouts. All Lua files pass parsing.
No live player-to-player test has been performed for this build.

This improves record propagation; it does not supply an authoritative realm
database or guarantee identical lists. Other clients still limit what they
send, disconnected peers cannot exchange data, and a full book can take
several exchanges. The change is most effective when other senders adopt it.

Character layout added in build 1:

The journal now keeps your character on the left third of the window. The
existing pages use the right two thirds, with all six bottom tabs preserved.
The window starts at 1140 x 620 and can be resized down to 1140 x 560.

The preview starts from your live character and applies selected, saved
fishing outfit pieces. Unchecked, unsaved, and saved-empty slots keep current
gear, matching the wardrobe's equip behavior. A saved fishing pole clears the
preview's off hand. The preview itself never equips or removes actual gear.
Drag the character to turn, scroll to zoom, or use Reset view. Outfit opens
the existing wardrobe. The preview refreshes when gear or outfit choices change.

Realm position above the character uses the addon's all-time record standings,
not the XP leaderboard or seasonal filter. These are the standings known from
shared records; hover for that context. Angler title and purple XP progress
appear below the character.

Godfall's existing community theme, artwork, widgets, and MIT attribution are
preserved. See LICENSE-UIRework.txt. This development build uses the existing
saved data and remains dormant while the original TheFishingLog is enabled.

Restart the client after installing this build to pick up the added Lua file.
Enable The Fishing Log (Test) with the original The Fishing Log disabled.
No data migration is needed.

Validation: all 86 Lua files parse as Lua 5.1; TOC entries and load order checked.
Mock-client checks cover outfit slot preservation, two-hand/off-hand behavior,
delayed loading and failures, event refresh, rank/XP, camera controls, journal
construction, bottom tabs, and workspace width. In-game rendering has not been
visually tested for this build.
